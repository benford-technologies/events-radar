# Who we are (used by the scanner to score relevance)

Benford Technologies is a tech-native audit firm with offices in London and Oslo.
We run statutory audits end-to-end on our own platform ("Audit OS"), which connects
to clients' accounting systems (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Visma, Tripletex, PowerOffice,
NetSuite, Dynamics) and bank data. Audit services are currently delivered in Norway
through Benford Revisjon AS; we are building our presence in the UK.

## Who we want to meet
- CFOs, finance directors, heads of finance, financial controllers ("økonomisjef", "finansdirektør")
  at scale-ups and mid-sized companies, especially VC/PE-backed.
- Accountants and accounting firms (partners for referrals).
- Founders and operators of fast-growing companies in the UK and Norway.
- Investors (VC/PE) whose portfolio companies need audits.
- Students and graduates in accounting, finance, CS and data (for hiring / employer brand).

## What makes an event relevant (score higher)
- Audience is finance leaders, accountants, auditors or founders.
- Located in London, Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, Stavanger or elsewhere in the UK / Norway / Nordics.
- Has a call for speakers, panel slots, sponsorship, or exhibitor options.
- Topics: audit, accounting, finance transformation, AI in finance, ERP, fintech, regtech, ESG reporting, scale-up finance.

## Score lower
- Purely consumer, crypto-trading, or retail-banking events.
- Events outside UK / Nordics unless they are major global finance/audit events.
- Webinars with no networking.
